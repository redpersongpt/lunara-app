Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Lunara is built so your sleep information stays with you. This policy explains what the app does with data.

What we collect

Very little, and only if you use the parts that need it. Lunara has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your sleep data never leaves your phone.

Optional account

You can use Lunara without an account. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google (to start your one free night, or to keep purchases when you change phones), our server receives the sign-in token from Apple or Google, checks it, and stores: a random account ID that we create, a one-way code (a keyed hash) of your Apple or Google account ID, and session records for staying signed in. We do not store your name or email address. If you use Sign in with Apple, we may also store an encrypted Apple token so we can revoke your Apple sign-in when you delete your account.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the You tab. This removes your account ID, sessions and purchase link from our server and from RevenueCat, and revokes Sign in with Apple. To stop one person from claiming the free night repeatedly, we keep the one-way code of the Apple or Google account ID and the date the free night was used, linked to no account and to nothing else. App Store subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting the account; cancel them in your Apple Account settings.

What stays on your phone

Your name or nickname (optional), bedtime and wake time, your wind-down plan, sleep diary entries, experiment notes, favorite sounds and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app. Sounds are generated on the device. You can export this data as a JSON file or erase it at any time from the You tab.

Notifications

If you turn on a wind-down reminder, Lunara schedules a local notification on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Lunara uses RevenueCat to check which plan is active: RevenueCat receives your purchase history for Lunara and an anonymous app user ID (or the random account ID if you signed in). RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. The free night is granted through RevenueCat as a 24-hour promotional entitlement.

Health data

Lunara does not connect to Apple Health and does not read data from your watch or other devices.

Children

Lunara is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue.
