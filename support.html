Support

Something not working, or have an idea? Open an issue and we'll get back to you.

Subscriptions

Lunara Premium is a weekly or monthly subscription billed by Apple. To cancel or change it, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open the paywall in Lunara and tap Restore Purchases.

Free version

You can use Lunara without subscribing. The nighttime SOS screen, your plan, the sleep diary, rain sounds and the first story are always free.

Your data

Everything is stored on your phone. To move it, use Export in the You tab. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Medical note

Lunara is a wellness app, not medical care. If sleeplessness is persistent, getting worse, or affecting your safety, please talk to a qualified healthcare professional.
